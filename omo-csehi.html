2.2. A

  • Several textbooks and study materials for Computer Science and Engineering in Hindi are available, catering to various academic levels and competitive exams. For higher education, titles like "C++ Bhasha mein programming" by S.S. Srivastava and "PC Architecture and Peripherals-II (Hindi Medium)" by Mohit Mathur are offered by publishers such as Laxmi Publications and Nageen Prakashan Other textbooks include "कॉंसेप्ट ऑफ़ प्रोग्रामिंग यूजिंग 'C'" by Yatendra Kumar and "कम्युनिकेशन इंजीनियरिंग के सिद्धान्त" by Y.D. Sharma, available through Kopykitab
  • For students pursuing BCA, PGDCA, Polytechnic, and other competitive exams, free downloadable PDFs are accessible. These include comprehensive notes on subjects like DBMS, Basic Computer, Java, Network Security, HTML, Data Structures, Software Engineering, SQL, CSS, JavaScript, Python, Data Mining, Operating Systems, and IoT, all presented in Hindi Specific resources like "Computer Parikshadham (Computer General Knowledge)" and "Pariksha Manthan Computer Ek Parichay" are highlighted as essential for exams like SSC, Banking, Railway, and various PSCs
  • Additionally, online marketplaces like Meesho offer physical books such as "Programming through Python (HINDI)" and "CCC Course on Computer Concepts Paperback" For B.Tech CSE students, reference books like "Computer Fundamentals and Programming in C" by Reema Thareja and "Fundamentals of Computer Science Engineering for RGPV" by A. P. Godse are recommended, though the availability of these in Hindi is not specified in the provided results
